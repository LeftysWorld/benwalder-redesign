The Ten-Series

Ten sessions. Your whole body. Change that lasts.

The most complete work Ben offers. Based on Dr. Ida Rolf's method, the Ten-Series reorganizes your body step by step, from the breath and outer layers, through the core, to a new, easier way of standing and moving.

Session by session

Each session builds on the last.

Three phases: free the outer “sleeve,” reorganize the core, then integrate it all.

  1. 1Sleeve

    Session 1: Breath

    Frees the outer layers around the ribs, shoulders and hips so breathing can open up.

  2. 2Sleeve

    Session 2: Foundation

    Feet and lower legs: building support from the ground up.

  3. 3Sleeve

    Session 3: Side line

    Balances front and back along the sides of the body.

  4. 4Core

    Session 4: Inside line

    Inner legs up to the pelvic floor.

  5. 5Core

    Session 5: Front core

    Abdomen and the deep hip flexors that link legs to spine.

  6. 6Core

    Session 6: Back core

    Back of the legs, sacrum and spine.

  7. 7Core

    Session 7: Head & neck

    Lets the head sit easily on top of the spine.

  8. 8Integration

    Session 8: Lower body

    Integrates the pelvis and legs into the new pattern.

  9. 9Integration

    Session 9: Upper body

    Integrates the shoulders, arms and ribs.

  10. 10Integration

    Session 10: Whole body

    Brings everything together into one coherent, easy way of moving.

Questions

About the Ten-Series.

How often are Ten-Series sessions?

Usually once a week or every other week, so your body has time to integrate each session before the next. Ben will help you find a rhythm that fits your life.

Do I have to commit to all ten?

You can start with Session 1 and decide from there. Many people choose the prepaid package after their first session because it saves money.

What if I miss a week?

That's fine. The series is flexible. A longer gap just means a little more time reconnecting at the start of the next session.

What happens after the series?

Most people simply enjoy the results. Some come back for occasional tune-ups, especially athletes or anyone with a physical job.
