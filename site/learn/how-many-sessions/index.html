How Many Structural Integration Sessions Do You Need?

Some people feel a difference in one session, others benefit from the full Ten-Series. Here's how to think about how many Structural Integration sessions you need.

Short answerIt depends on your goals and how long the pattern has been there. Many people notice a change in one to three sessions. Longstanding pain, old injuries and posture issues usually respond best to a series, and the classic Ten-Series is designed for comprehensive, lasting change.

One to three sessions

A good starting point if you want to feel what the work does, or if you have a fairly recent, specific issue. Many people notice they breathe, stand or move differently after the first session.

A short series (four to six sessions)

Useful when there's a clear area of concern, like an old ankle injury affecting your knee and hip, and you want to work through the pattern around it.

The Ten-Series

The most complete option. It works through the whole body in a planned sequence, which is why it's often recommended for pain or posture problems that have been around for years. See how the Ten-Series works.

No pressure either way

Ben checks progress with you as you go and won't push you toward more sessions than you need.
