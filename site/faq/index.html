Who is this work for?

Anyone who wants individualized, hands-on care for their body. You don't need to be flexible, athletic or know anything about bodywork. People most often come in for chronic pain, old injuries, recovery from surgery or accidents, posture, tension or stress. All ages and backgrounds are welcome.

Is this the same as massage?

Not exactly. It's usually slower and more specific than massage, and it looks at long-term patterns across your whole body rather than just relaxation or temporary relief in one spot.

What is Structural Integration?

Also called Rolfing, it's hands-on bodywork that treats the body as one connected system. It works mainly with fascia, the connective tissue that links everything, to improve posture, mobility, breathing and comfort over time.

Is the work painful?

It can feel intense in areas that have been restricted for a long time, but Ben works responsively rather than forcefully. You're always in control of the pressure, and most people find it manageable and relieving.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on your goals. Some people feel a meaningful change in a few sessions; others choose ongoing work like the Ten-Series for longstanding patterns. You'll decide together, with no pressure toward a long commitment.

How much does it cost?

First sessions are $___ (90 minutes), single sessions are $___, and the Ten-Series package is $____. See pricing for every option.

Is this covered by insurance?

Usually not by standard health insurance. Ben can provide receipts and documentation you can submit for possible reimbursement, and HSA/FSA cards are often accepted for this kind of care. Check with your plan.

What should I wear?

Comfortable clothing you can move in. Shorts and a tank top or sports bra work well. Ben may ask you to adjust clothing for access to certain areas, always based on your comfort and preferences.

How long is a session?

First sessions are about 90 minutes, which includes time to talk through your history. Follow-up sessions are usually 60–75 minutes.

Can I do this alongside physical therapy or chiropractic?

Yes, and many clients do. Ben's work often makes other care more effective. He works alongside your medical team, not instead of it.

Do you offer herbal consultations online?

Yes. Herbal and nutritional consultations can be done in person or online, so you don't need to be in San Diego.

I'm still not sure this is for me.

That's completely fine. Skeptics and people who aren't sure what they need are welcome. Book a free 15-minute call and talk it through with Ben first.
