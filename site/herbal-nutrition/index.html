Questions

About consultations.

Can I do a consultation online?

Yes. Consultations work just as well by video, so you can book from anywhere.

Do I have to buy herbs from you?

No. Ben can make custom formulations if you'd like, but he'll also help you source herbs from a grocery store, a trusted supplier or your own garden.

Is this safe with my medications?

Tell Ben about every medication and supplement you take. He'll factor that in, and recommend checking with your doctor or pharmacist where there could be an interaction.

Is this a diet?

No rigid diets. The focus is practical: meal structure, blood sugar, digestion, hydration and nourishing habits you can actually keep.

Herbal and nutritional consultations are educational and supportive. They are not a substitute for medical diagnosis or treatment.
