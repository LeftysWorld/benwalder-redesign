Approaches

Craniosacral therapy: gentle work for a nervous system that won't settle.

A slower, subtler approach that uses light, still contact to help the nervous system ease out of stress, guarding and overload. Most people find it deeply calming.

What it is

How it works.

Craniosacral therapy works with the membranes and fluid around the brain and spinal cord, and the bones of the skull, spine and sacrum.

Ben uses very light, patient contact, often at the head, neck, spine or pelvis, to support your body's own ability to release tension and settle.

He often weaves it into structural sessions when the body needs to calm down before deeper work can land.

Often helpful for
  • Stress and tension held in the body
  • Jaw clenching and neck tension
  • Recovery after accidents or surgery
  • Feeling wired, tired or disconnected
  • People sensitive to deep pressure
What it feels like

Very gentle. Most people feel warmth, softening or deep relaxation, and some drift toward sleep.

Ben combines approaches within a session based on what your body needs, so you don't have to choose.
