Conditions

When stress lives in your body and won't let go.

A clenched jaw, shoulders up by your ears, breath that stops at your chest, a body that can't seem to rest. Ben's gentler work helps your nervous system remember how to settle.

Does this sound like you?

If you're nodding along, you're in the right place.

  • You clench your jaw or grind your teeth.
  • Your breathing feels shallow or stuck high in your chest.
  • Your shoulders and neck are tight no matter what you do.
  • You feel tired but wired, especially at night.
  • Massage feels nice but doesn't really reach it.
  • You feel disconnected from your body.
Why it persists

Why it keeps happening.

Long periods of stress teach the body to stay braced: muscles guard, breathing gets shallow and the nervous system stays switched on. Over time, that becomes the new normal.

Because it's driven by the nervous system, pressing harder on tight muscles rarely changes it for long.

Ben uses slower, subtler approaches that speak to the nervous system directly, alongside breath work, so your body can shift out of protection and into rest.

Questions

Common questions about this.

Is this therapy for anxiety?

Ben doesn't treat mental health conditions, and this isn't a replacement for counseling or medical care. Many people find that calming, body-based work helps them feel more settled alongside that support.

Is craniosacral work deep or light?

Light. It uses gentle, still contact rather than deep pressure, which is why many people find it so relaxing.

Can herbs help with stress and sleep?

Ben offers consultations on herbs, food and daily habits that support rest and resilience. It's educational and personalized, and he'll check for anything that might interact with your medications.
