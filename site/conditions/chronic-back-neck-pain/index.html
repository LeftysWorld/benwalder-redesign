Why it persists

Why it keeps happening.

Pain that keeps returning is often a sign of compensation. Your body is very good at protecting a sore area by shifting load somewhere else: a slightly rotated pelvis, a ribcage that stopped moving, a neck that took over the work.

Treating only the painful spot relieves it for a while. But the surrounding pattern pulls it straight back. That's why the relief doesn't last.

Ben's work is aimed at the pattern: freeing the fascia and joints that are holding the compensation in place, so the painful area doesn't have to keep working overtime.

Questions

Common questions about this.

Can bodywork help back pain when physical therapy didn't?

It often complements it. PT strengthens and retrains; Ben's work releases the fascia and joint restrictions that can keep pulling you back into the old pattern. Many clients find the two work better together.

Is it safe if I have a disc issue or past back surgery?

Ben adapts the work to your history and works gently around sensitive areas. Bring any imaging or surgical notes to your first visit, and check with your doctor if you're unsure.

How is this different from a deep tissue massage?

Deep tissue focuses on tight muscles in the area that hurts. Ben works more slowly and specifically, and across the whole body, aiming at the pattern that keeps recreating the tension.
