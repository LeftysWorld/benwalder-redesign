Conditions

Posture that won't hold, and movement that feels harder than it should.

You can't stretch or will your way into better posture if the tissue holding you there won't let go. Ben works with the structure itself, so better alignment becomes the easy default.

  • You correct your posture, and a minute later you're slumped again.

  • You feel older in your body than you are.
Why it persists

Why it keeps happening.

Posture isn't just a habit. It's held in place by fascia, the connective tissue that wraps and links every muscle. Over years of desk work, injuries and stress, it adapts to the shapes you spend the most time in.

That's why trying harder to sit up straight doesn't last: you're fighting the tissue instead of changing it.

Structural Integration works with that tissue directly, layer by layer, so your body can find a more upright, easier way to be without constant effort.

Questions

Common questions about this.

Can bodywork actually change posture?

Structural Integration is designed for exactly this. By releasing and reorganizing the fascia that holds habitual shapes, it can make a more balanced posture feel natural instead of effortful.

Do I need to do exercises at home?

Ben may suggest a few simple movement or awareness practices, but there's no heavy homework. The goal is to make good posture feel easier, not to add another routine.

Is the Ten-Series necessary for posture?

Not always. Some people get what they need from a few sessions. The Ten-Series is the most complete option if your posture has been a long-term issue.
